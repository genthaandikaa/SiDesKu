# Color Palette & Structured Gradients (Flowora Modern SaaS Style)

Pembaruan palet warna dan sistem gradasi terstruktur yang diadopsi dari referensi modern SaaS (Flowora style) dengan basis warna biru muda (Sky Blue) yang harmonis:

## 1. Primary Light Blue & Gradients
- **Sky Light Base:** `#f0f9ff` (`sky-50`), `#e0f2fe` (`sky-100`)
- **Sky Accent:** `#38bdf8` (`sky-400`), `#0ea5e9` (`sky-500`), `#0284c7` (`sky-600`)
- **Royal Azure / Indigo Accent:** `#2563eb` (`blue-600`), `#1d4ed8` (`blue-700`), `#4f46e5` (`indigo-600`)
- **Text Gradient Utama:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #0ea5e9 0%, #2563eb 50%, #4f46e5 100%)`
- **Button & Banner Gradient:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #38bdf8 0%, #2563eb 50%, #4f46e5 100%)`

## 2. Background Canvas & Ambient Lighting
- **Canvas Base:** Pristine White `#ffffff` ke soft sky gradient `#f0f9ff`/30%
- **Dot Grid Matrix:** `radial-gradient(#0ea5e9 1px, transparent 1px)` opacity 0.08, size 28px
- **Ambient Radial Orbs:** Soft glowing blurs (sky-400/20%, blue-500/10%, indigo-400/10%)

## 3. Surface & Card Design
- **Card Background:** `bg-white/95 backdrop-blur-xl`
- **Border:** `border-sky-100` hover `border-sky-300`
- **Card Radius:** `rounded-3xl`
- **Elevation / Shadow:** `shadow-xl shadow-sky-500/5`
- **Pastel Icon Squircles:**
  - Sky: `bg-sky-50 text-sky-600 border border-sky-100`
  - Indigo: `bg-indigo-50 text-indigo-600 border border-indigo-100`
  - Emerald: `bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-600 border border-emerald-100`
  - Blue: `bg-blue-50 text-blue-600 border border-blue-100`
